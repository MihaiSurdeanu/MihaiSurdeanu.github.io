About

I work on natural language processing, building computer systems that process and extract meaning from natural language texts.

I am particularly interested in neuro-symbolic approaches that combine neural systems, such as large language models, with interpretable symbolic methods, such as deterministic rules.

Research interests

  • Natural language processing
  • Large language models
  • Neuro-symbolic AI
  • Machine reasoning
  • Information extraction
